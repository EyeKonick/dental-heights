# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static site at the project root (no JS framework; output in `dist/`).

## Users

People in and around Cebu City looking for a dentist, most often on a phone, who want to know quickly what the clinic does, where it is, and how to get an appointment. Many will prefer to call or send a Facebook Messenger message instead of filling in a form.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Dental Heights, a general and cosmetic dental clinic. Success is a booked appointment ("Book Now", wording taken from the clinic's own flyer). Secondary actions: call 0927 034 8411, message the clinic on Facebook Messenger, see the services list.

## Positioning

A local Cebu City clinic with a recognizable team (they appear in all clinic photos) and a mountain/peak identity: the name "Heights", the tagline, and alpine mountain murals on the clinic walls.

## Operating Context

- Address: Unit 203, 2nd floor, Iconique Center, Colon St., Cebu City (Philippines 6000).
- Phone: 0927 034 8411. Email (from contact screenshot): dentalheightscompany@gmail.com.
- Facebook page: "Dental Heights". The page handle is unknown, so the Messenger link is a placeholder (`https://m.me/`) with a TODO.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services (from the clinic flyer, IMG_2244): dental consultation, dental cleaning, filling/pasta, tooth extraction, braces, root canal therapy, dental implants, direct veneers, jacket crowns, teeth whitening, dentures, wisdom tooth extraction.

Undecided / unknown, so never invent: opening hours, prices, review counts, testimonials, doctor names, credentials.

## Brand Commitments

- Tagline, used word for word: "Reaching the peak of your confidence, one smile at a time."
- Logo (IMG_2252, web copy in `src/assets/images/brand/logo.png`) used as provided (white background, so it sits on a white or light surface). No recoloring, redrawing or recreating it.
- Logo colors: sky blue, sunny yellow, cool grey. Mountain/peak theme.
- Site palette follows the clinic's Facebook posts (user direction, 2026-11-17): ice-blue grounds, bright sky-blue fields, deep navy text, white cards, yellow kept for a single Book Now.
- One Book Now on the whole page (user direction, 2026-11-17); every other action is call, Messenger or directions.
- Primary CTA label "Book Now", matching the flyer.

## Product Principles

1. Booking is always one tap away. Calling and Messenger come right after it.
2. Show the real team and the real clinic. No stock models.
3. Only state facts the clinic has given us.
4. The peak/height idea carries the brand, and stays quiet rather than turning into a gimmick.
