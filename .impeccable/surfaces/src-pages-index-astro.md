# Surface: Dental Heights landing page (Astro, src/pages/index.astro)

Scope: the whole one-page site: hero, services, about, real smiles gallery, location/contact, footer. Mode: Persuade. Audience: Cebu patients arriving from Facebook on phones. Primary action: Book Now (yellow, per the user). Tap-to-call is always one tap away on phones.

World: inherited from the approved impeccable Warm Cebu Care hero (the impeccable Warm Cebu Care bake-off build, since removed). This is an extension of an established world, so there is no concept roll. User changes: Book Now is logo yellow (#f5d65a) with cocoa text, and sky blue stays for links and secondary accents.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The neighbourhood clinic you already know by face. Every section is carried by the real team and real results, never by illustration or stock. It refuses the clinical white-and-blue template.
OWN-WORLD: (revised 2026-11-17 from the clinic's Facebook posts) Ice-blue ground (#e9f6ff), bright sky-blue fields and tilted stack cards (#3ab6ff), deep navy ink (#0c3268), white rounded panels (radius 26–40), sky-blue rings on secondary pills, and one yellow pill for the single Book Now in the hero. Gabarito display, Rethink Sans text. The peak motif appears only as a single authored ridge line.
STORY: Meet the team (hero), see exactly what they treat (services in six plain groups, each with Book Now), know who they are (about, mural team photo), see proof (real smiles, honestly captioned), find the 2nd-floor clinic (location), book (footer).
FIRST VIEWPORT: Identical to the approved hero: tagline h1 left, Book Now (yellow) + Call pill, Messenger link, address; team on a white panel over a tilted tan card on the right; "Real smiles" inset at the lower left of the panel. Phone: stacked, with a fixed Tap to call / Book Now bar.
FORM: Extension of the approved Warm Cebu Care hero. seed: none (pinned): "Use the 'Warm Cebu Care' version in bakeoff.html … as a visual reference for the hero … Then build the body in Astro in that same style."
Signature interaction: the smile inset fans out on load and swings apart on hover; the gallery smile tiles do the same small swing. Section motion is one gentle rise on first view, content visible by default.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
